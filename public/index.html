Core App Development Services

The difference between an app that launches and an app that lasts lies in how it is built. These core services cover the architecture, scalability, and performance choices that define your product's future.

01

Strategy & Consulting

You've got the idea. But… which tech? What stack? How much to build? That's where our Strategy Sprint comes in. We define scope, surface risks, and turn ambiguity into an executable plan aligned to ROI from day one.

+

Analysis & Requirements

Apps fail when they're built on assumptions. We map your reality — how your business runs, where users get stuck, and which fixes will move the needle. We define features, map user flows, flag dependencies, and give you total clarity on what's being built and why.

MVP Planning & Roadmap

You don't need every feature on day one. We nail down your Minimum Viable Product — the "must-haves" that validate your market and deliver ROI fast. Then we build a roadmap that's practical, flexible, and speeds up launch without killing your long-term vision.

Competitive & Market Research

We benchmark your product against competitors not just to match them — but to outsmart them. We dive into user reviews, app store data, UI patterns, and feature gaps across the competitive landscape. We map out what's working, what's failing, and what users are still asking for.

Tech Stack & Architecture

Choosing tech isn't about trends, it's about smart trade-offs. We'll cut through the noise and tell you which one fits your app's goals — faster UIs, deeper OS-level features, or both. We make the tough calls simple so you can build right from day one.

02

UI/UX Design

From clear user flows to subtle animations, we build experiences that guide, not confuse. Your users won't just navigate — they'll glide.

+

Wireframing & Prototyping

This is where concepts take shape. We start with low-fidelity wireframes that lay out the core flows, screen hierarchy, and user decisions — for validation. You'll see exactly how a new user signs up and how a feature is accessed. This gives you a feel for the actual experience — on device, not just in theory.

Platform-Specific Interface Design

Ever opened an Android app that felt like it was designed for iPhone and everything just felt… off? That's what we avoid at all costs. We follow Apple's Human Interface Guidelines and Google's Material Design to the letter, while still bringing in your brand identity. That means native gestures, intuitive navigation, and design patterns that just feel "right" to you and your users.

Interaction Design & Micro-Animations

The delight is in the details. Every shimmer, swipe, and button response is crafted to feel intentional. These micro-interactions don't just look good — they reduce friction, give feedback, and make your app feel alive.

Accessibility & Usability

Great design works for everyone. We follow WCAG and ADA standards to support users with visual, motor, or cognitive challenges because if your app isn't inclusive, it's incomplete.

03

Mobile App Development

Mobile apps that lag by seconds lose users by the minute. We help you choose the right build — native, cross-platform, or PWA — and develop apps that load fast, scale smoothly, and support next-gen tech like AI, AR, and 5G.

+

Native iOS Development (Swift, Objective-C)

If you're building for Apple users and want smooth performance, best-in-class battery optimization, and full access to iOS features like Face ID, Haptics, and ARKit, going native is the smartest move. We use Swift for most modern builds, with Objective-C support for legacy systems, ensuring your app runs flawlessly across iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch.

Native Android Development (Kotlin)

When it comes to Android, going native means more control, better performance, and the flexibility to build for every screen size, OS version, and chipset out there. We use Kotlin for most modern builds, not just because it's Google's preferred language, but because it lets us write cleaner, more expressive code with less boilerplate.

Cross-Platform Development (Flutter, React Native, Xamarin)

"Cross-platform apps are not fast enough." That was true until frameworks like Flutter, React Native, and Xamarin came into play. Today, you can launch high-performance apps that feel native, tap into platform-specific features, and still share a unified codebase across iOS and Android. With us, you get a cross-platform build that feels tailored to each device, performs at speed, and cuts your time to market in half.

Progressive Web Apps (PWAs)

Feature-rich websites or mobile apps? Not sure which one to choose? Get the best of both worlds. Build a progressive web app (PWA) that starts fast, stays fast, runs without the internet, works in any browser, installs on any device, and is responsive to any screen size.

04

Backend & API Development

We develop the backend to match how your mobile app actually functions in the hands of real users. Whether it's choosing the right architecture (microservices or monolith), integrating RESTful or GraphQL APIs, or optimizing databases like MongoDB, PostgreSQL, or Firebase — our focus is always on performance and reliability.

+

GraphQL API Integration

Imagine your mobile app currently pulls extensive user data for a simple login screen, where you only need the user's first name and email. We fix this with GraphQL. Save bandwidth for your mobile users with limited data plans and save yourself by adjusting data requests without backend overhauls. Build fast apps for mobile, smartwatches, and IoT devices, where bandwidth usage matters.

RESTful API Integration

Need to add a new feature to your app? With well-structured RESTful APIs and a modular backend, we make it quick to extend functionality without rebuilding the whole system. By designing endpoints that are predictable, reusable, and secure, we ensure new features and updates can be rolled out rapidly without breaking existing workflows.

Node.js

While others are still stuck at creating single-page web applications with Node.js, we are advancing in real-time applications like messaging, audio or video streaming apps that handle significant data loads, and scalable applications like Uber that can manage high peak loads efficiently. From web scraping to setting up third-party integrations, we use Node.js to develop feature-rich apps to support large numbers of users.

Django

Frustrated by repetitive backend tasks that slow down mobile development? Django's "batteries-included" philosophy speeds things up with built-in components that let us move fast without cutting corners. Whether it's handling user authentication, admin controls, or data-heavy workflows, Django helps us deliver backend logic that's reliable, maintainable, and ready to scale as your app grows.

Microservices Architecture

What if you want to make a small feature change in your app? Will it risk app downtime? We build apps with microservice architecture by breaking one massive backend that tries to do everything into smaller, independent services. So that you can always add or remove a new feature without making a significant change in your backend. With microservices, we make your backend flexible, fault-tolerant, and built for scale.

Scalable Databases (MongoDB, PostgreSQL, and Firebase)

Let's say your app pulls in live social media feeds, manages user uploads, or processes real-time sensor data. Now multiply that by 50,000 users doing it all at once. That's where the right database architecture makes or breaks your app. To ensure your app doesn't freeze during peak times, we'll make sure it can handle data integration from dissimilar sources, high-speed data ingestion, and low-latency performance with scalable databases like MongoDB, PostgreSQL, and Firebase.

05

Quality Assurance

A crash. A lag. A button that doesn't respond. That's all it takes for users to uninstall your app — and we're here to make sure that never happens. Our QA process blends manual and automated testing to catch issues early and often, from functionality bugs to edge-case behavior.

+

Manual & Automated Testing

Need to evaluate how user-friendly your app is? It involves assessing the overall UX — intuitiveness of the UI, navigation, and design — aspects that only human testers can effectively gauge. That's why we simulate real-world scenarios to discover usability, functionality, and exploratory issues. At the same time, we automate regression tests, routine checks, and backend validations — with tools like Appium, NightwatchJS, Calabash, and XCUITest so we can cover more ground, catch more bugs, and release faster.

Device & OS Compatibility Testing

Your app might run flawlessly on the device in your hand — but what about a tablet from 2020, or a mid-range Android with an older OS? Compatibility issues don't just frustrate users; they lead to instant uninstalls. That's why we go beyond emulators and perform device & OS compatibility testing on your app on real devices across a wide range of screen sizes, resolutions, and OS.

Security & Performance Testing

Your users expect your app to load under 2 seconds and protect their privacy at every interaction. We make sure your app does all. We stress-test your app under heavy usage, identify performance blockers, and optimize for speed across network conditions. At the same time, we audit for security flaws — checking API endpoints, authentication logic, data storage practices, and potential vulnerabilities that could expose user data.

Continuous QA Through CI/CD

QA isn't something that is added at the end of your mobile app development cycle. We integrate that into every step of your app development. We integrate continuous testing through CI/CD pipelines into every step of the build to make sure your app stays stable, secure, and release-ready at every stage. Every time a new feature is added, or code is updated — whether it's a new splash screen or a new AI feature — automated tests run immediately.

06

DevOps

Need to add a new add-on feature to your mobile app based on new user trends? With VE, you don't have to wait for several months to roll out new features and updates. Our developers, quality assurance (QA), and operations teams work together using the DevOps toolchain and test-driven development (TDD) methodology to accelerate the process.

+

AWS, GCP, Azure Integration

Imagine you're running a wellness app, and suddenly, hundreds of users start uploading reels after a major influencer shoutout. Your backend needs to process uploads, stream videos, and scale instantly. That's where smart cloud integration comes in — auto-scaling to handle traffic surges, and dynamic storage that grows with demand. We plug in everything from Firebase to AWS AI tools, so your app stays smooth, stable, and ready — no matter how fast it scales.

CI/CD Pipelines with GitHub Actions & Bitrise

You want to roll out new features fast, but not at the cost of breaking something that was working perfectly yesterday. That's exactly where our CI/CD setup comes into play. The moment you approve a new feature or request a fix, your app immediately runs through automated testing and staging — powered by GitHub Actions and Bitrise. No waiting for manual builds. No wondering if it broke something else.

Docker & Kubernetes Deployment

When your app suddenly scales — like during a flash sale or viral spike — Docker and Kubernetes keep everything stable. We set up containerized environments with auto-scaling, rolling updates, and zero-downtime deployments, so your backend stays resilient while your users experience a fast, seamless front-end without interruptions or delays, no matter the load.

Monitoring with Crashlytics, New Relic & Prometheus

Once your app goes live, the real work begins — keeping it stable, fast, and error-free. We integrate tools like Crashlytics to catch crashes in real time. New Relic gives us deep performance insights on response times and memory usage. And we use Prometheus to monitor system health, uptime, and resource allocation. Together, these tools give us a full picture of how your app behaves in the real world — helping us troubleshoot faster.

07

Security & Compliance

Users trust your app with their data, and we make sure that trust is earned and protected. We implement secure development practices: encrypted data storage, secure API communication (HTTPS/TLS), and token-based authentication to keep sensitive info safe.

+

Data Encryption (AES-256, RSA)

We use AES-256 encryption — the same level trusted by banks — to encrypt anything stored on a device or in the cloud. Think of it like turning every piece of stored data into an unsolvable puzzle unless you've got the exact key. Every time your app sends data, we secure that data in motion using RSA encryption and SSL/TLS protocols. Whether it's login credentials, health records, or payment info, your users' data is protected at every step — at rest, in transit, and everywhere in between.

OAuth 2.0 / JWT Authentication

Let's be honest, no one wants to remember another password. That's why the best apps let you log in with your Google, Apple, or Facebook account in just a tap. That's OAuth 2.0 in action. We build that right into your app. And once a user is verified, we hand them a secure session token (called a JWT) that travels with each request. Whether you're building a social app that lets users sign in with Facebook or an enterprise tool that manages employee access across devices, we make sure your login system is smooth for users and safe for you.

GDPR & HIPAA Compliance

When someone downloads your app, they're not just trusting you with their data — they're trusting you to follow the rules. And depending on where your users are, those rules can be serious. Think: a user in Germany asking to delete their data forever (GDPR), or a US clinic storing patient notes on a mobile dashboard (HIPAA). If your app doesn't handle that right, you're risking legal trouble. We build compliance into your app from the start, giving you the confidence to grow in strict markets like the EU or US healthcare without second-guessing if your app is regulation-ready.

Penetration Testing & Vulnerability Scans

Small security gaps can become big risks — weak login flows, exposed APIs, unsafe forms, outdated libraries, or poorly configured servers. We test your app to find these issues before attackers do. Our team combines manual penetration testing with automated vulnerability scans to detect risks like SQL injection, cross-site scripting, insecure headers, exposed endpoints, and weak configurations. Every issue is reported clearly, with its severity and recommended fix.

FAQ

The Usual App Problems, Solved

What if your app project gets stuck?+

It won't. Every mobile project is supported by a full-service delivery pod — architects, developers, QA, DevOps, and backups — so when one piece slows down, the system doesn't. We route challenges to the right specialists, solve faster, and keep the build moving.

What if a policy update threatens app stability?+

We see it before it hits. Our mobile service model includes proactive compliance monitoring — whether it's Play Store policy, iOS privacy updates, or SDK version changes. We adapt your codebase early to avoid post-launch chaos.

What if your app starts slowing down as users grow?+

It won't be left behind. We optimize performance from day one using real-device testing, backend load balancing, and monitoring tools like Crashlytics and New Relic. If usage spikes or features scale, your app keeps running smooth without retrofitting under pressure.

What if your app crashes on certain devices?+

It gets fixed before users notice. We test across real devices — not just emulators — covering a range of OS versions, screen sizes, and hardware specs. Any crash logs are monitored live and patched through zero-downtime deployments.

What if timelines slip mid-project?+

They won't go unnoticed or unchecked. Every mobile project follows milestone-based tracking, with weekly check-ins, burn-down charts, and early-warning systems. If delays loom, we reallocate bandwidth or adjust scope collaboratively before timelines are at risk.

What if your app is ready but the backend isn't?+

We decouple delivery paths. Frontend, backend, and integrations run in parallel where possible. And if a dependency slows down, we sandbox features or use simulators — so your mobile app stays on track.
